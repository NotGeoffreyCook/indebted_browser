Unclaimed // Redeemer

ED

Everything he owns is broken. Everything he was issued is not.
Body
Bare exoskeleton with the nervous system running through it. Left arm and right shin have no covering. The other plates are secondhand, chipped and taped, each from a different brand: RED thigh, BLU arm, GLD chest, with a few white plates mixed in. Over that, a sleeveless puffer hanging open and khaki cargo shorts. The head stays unbranded unless he picks a brand hat.
Face
The default smiley anyone gets if the big four never claimed them. Cracked at both eyes, with one crack running down the cheek like a tear.
Read
Scuffed and mismatched is his. Black and clean was issued, including the Redeemer ID with his photo. The hands and head should look like they cost more than the rest of him.
My calls, yours to overrule
  • The mask is off-white, not yellow. A yellow smiley would read as GLD, and Ed is meant to be unclaimed.
  • The interface covers the eye cracks. You only see them with it off. Worn, he keeps the smile and the tear, which comes out from under the unit.
  • The interface has eyes. Every headset front carries two large digital eyes with a drifting pupil and a blink every few seconds. On the black glass they're white discs with black rings; on the white ad screen they're black discs with white rings.
  • The nerves are the only warm colour on him. They are the human part, so they sit outside the grey scale and the brands.
  • "FINAL NOTICE", "Your ad here", "FINAL NOTICE" and the stencils are placeholder copy.
