      Controller: press any button to connect.

      The hub has four kiosks: paper, gun, market and rules. The range is ahead, with one target per brand. The course is through the east door, and the store, with every shelf item shootable, is through the north door. The enemy floor is through the west door: its spawn kiosk sends in one enemy at a time, RED, GLD, BLU, then GRN. Health heals on your provider's plan and bills you per HP; at 0 HP you pick a loan or pay the Medical Bill. The market opens when you fire, spawn an enemy or leave the hub. Floor grid squares are 1 m. On a touch screen, drag the left side to move and the right side to look.

      Health and loans

      Your provider is your health plan: it sets what starts regen, how fast it runs and when the bill lands. Kills pay cash. Spec numbers are fixed; these sliders are placeholders.
